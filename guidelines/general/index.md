# General guidelines

These language-independent guidelines apply to most software work. Read them
before a language-specific set: language guidance supplements this baseline
rather than replacing it. The guidelines are a starting point for discussion,
not a substitute for domain expertise or the needs of the people affected by a
system.

why it matters, and an example of applying it.
